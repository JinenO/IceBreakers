# IRIS FLOW: Final Stage Competition Pitch Script

### Slide 1: The Problem (The "Gap" in Assistive Tech)
*   **Hook**: "Traditional eye tracking costs $15,000. It requires perfect lighting and fixed posture. In an ICU, these aren't just 'challenges'—they are barriers to communication."
*   **Pain Point**: The 'Midas Touch' problem. If you look at something, you click it. It's exhausting.

### Slide 2: The Solution (IRIS FLOW)
*   **The Paradigm Shift**: We stop tracking *where* the patient looks to click. We use **Single-Switch Scanning**.
*   **Hardware**: Standard webcam. Cost? **$0**.
*   **Calibration**: Zero. It works the moment you open the laptop.

### Slide 3: Explosive Feature #1 - Quadrant-Aware Scanning
*   **The WOW Factor**: "Our AI doesn't just wait for you. It **predicts**."
*   **Tech**: By tracking the patient's head pose, the system identifies the quadrant the user is facing and **boosts scanning speed by 50%** in that area.
*   **Benefit**: Reduces communication fatigue by cutting selection time in half.

### Slide 4: Explosive Feature #2 - Patient Well-being Index (BVB)
*   **Human-Centric AI**: "We don't just send messages; we monitor the patient's state."
*   **Tech**: We analyze **Blink Variability (BVB)**. Rapid, erratic blinks signal stress. We sync this "Well-being Index" in real-time to the caregiver's mobile app.
*   **Impact**: Caregivers can intervene *before* an emergency happens.

### Slide 5: Explosive Feature #3 - ICU Night Mode
*   **Detail-Oriented**: "Lighting in the ICU changes. At night, a bright screen is painful and disrupts the patient's circadian rhythm."
*   **Action**: Our ambient light sensor triggers a **high-contrast Red/Black mode**. It's low-glare, protects melatonin production, and preserves the caregiver's night vision.

### Slide 6: The Vision
*   **Scale**: Iris Flow can be deployed to every ward in the world with a single URL.
*   **Closing**: "We are giving a voice to the voiceless, not with expensive hardware, but with smarter algorithms."
